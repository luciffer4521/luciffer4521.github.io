About · 关于我

大家好，我是刘慧敏。目前在东北大学软件学院泛虚拟现实人才培养与技术创新中心攻读硕士学位，同时担任科研助理。

Hello, I’m Huimin Liu, a master’s student and research assistant at the School of Software, Northeastern University, working with the Pan-Virtual Reality Talent Cultivation and Technological Innovation Center.

我的研究聚焦于视觉基础模型驱动的三维内容生成与结构化场景理解，涵盖三维高斯场景中的物体提取与几何解耦、视觉语言协同的遥感指代分割，以及提示驱动的拓扑矢量化与结构化几何解码。

My research focuses on visual foundation model–driven 3D content generation and structured scene understanding, including object extraction and geometry disentanglement in 3D Gaussian scenes, vision-language coordination for referring segmentation in remote sensing, and prompt-driven topological vectorization and structured geometry decoding.
